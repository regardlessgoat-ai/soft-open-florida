About this list

The Florida DBPR Division of Hotels and Restaurants posts public files of plan reviews and new licences. A script reads those files and makes these pages. No person edits the rows.

What "coming soon" means: a kitchen plan is on file in the last 120 days and there is no licence yet. Some places open in weeks, some in a year, some never.

What is not shown: no phone, no email and no owner names on the free pages.

Wrong row? The state record is the source. A contact address will be put here before this site is promoted.

Source: public licence records of the Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation (DBPR), Division of Hotels and Restaurants. This is not a state website. A plan on file is not a promise to open: dates and names can change. Business facts only. How this list is made and how to ask for a fix.
